Meta app compliance

Data Deletion Instructions

The Life Skills Meta app is used only to manage advertising for the organization’s own Meta advertising account. It does not maintain a Meta user-profile database or store Facebook or Instagram profile data, messages, friend lists, or user content.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to the verified public Life Skills contact below with the words “Meta data deletion request.”
  2. Include only the Meta account or advertising-account information needed to identify the request. Do not send sensitive personal information.
  3. We will confirm whether Life Skills holds any Meta platform data related to the request. If data under our control exists, we will delete it and confirm completion.

What normally happens

Because Life Skills does not store Meta user data in the Bnei Neviim Academy website or application database, there is normally no Meta user data for us to delete. Meta may separately retain information under its own policies when its platform is used.

Request channel: contact Life Skills on WhatsApp.

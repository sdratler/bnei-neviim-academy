Meta app compliance

Privacy Policy

Life Skills uses its Meta developer app only to manage advertising for its own organization-owned Meta advertising account. It is an administrator-side advertising integration; it is not a client portal and does not offer Facebook or Instagram Login.

Data the integration processes

For an authorized advertising action, the integration may process the Meta advertising-account and business-asset identifiers, and the campaign, ad set, ad, status, and policy information that Meta returns for that action. The purpose is limited to preparing, managing, and checking Life Skills advertising in the organization’s own advertising account.

Data the integration does not use or store

The Life Skills Meta app does not request or store Facebook or Instagram profile data, friend lists, messages, user content, or a Meta user database. The Bnei Neviim Academy website and its application database do not contain Meta Graph or Marketing API code or credentials for this integration, and do not retain Meta platform data. The integration is not used to process sensitive personal information.

Retention and sharing

Life Skills does not retain Meta platform data in the Bnei Neviim Academy website or application database. Meta may retain information under its own policies when its platform is used. We do not sell Meta platform data or use it for any purpose outside the organization’s own advertising management.

Your choices

If you believe this Meta app has processed data about you, or you have a privacy question, contact Life Skills through the verified public WhatsApp contact below. We will review the request and respond about any Meta platform data under our control.

Data deletion: See the Life Skills Meta data deletion instructions.
